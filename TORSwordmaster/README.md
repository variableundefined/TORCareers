# Swordmaster Career

*Trained in the ancient art of Sword-Dancing, you perform a form of High Magic with an enchanted sword, passed down as a family heirloom in your family. Your forms are similar, and an older form of the same martial art that are taught to the famous Swordmasters of Hoeth in Ulthuan.*

**Concept:** Based heavily on the Swordmaster of Hoeth, and the WFRP 4e Swordmaster career, riding on a single line - "the Way of the Sword is taught primarily to those in the Order of Swordmasters, it is believed there are reclusive High Elf colonies where remnants of the ancient art are still taught."

**Premise:** Melee focused career. Pseudo caster akin to a warrior priest, without sustain. Powerful personal fantasy and power. A follow on from Council Guard.

**Structure:** 1 - 3 - 3 to emphasize mid late power. Runelord uses the same shape.

**Career Ability:** Way of the Sword

Enter a trance in which you and your blade move as one. Gain +15% Physical Melee Damage, +15% Swing Speed and your Techniques, which spend charge. Charge drains 3% per second; the trance ends when it runs out. Gain charge from melee damage and blocks, including shield blocks (5%, once per second); perfect parries, chambers and deflections grant more. Requires a melee weapon. Each point in your highest melee skill reduces the drain by 0.005% per second.

- Requires full charge to enter. Entering refills the bar, so the charge is your technique pool for the trance.
- The trance lasts until charge runs out, you put away your melee weapon, or you use Way of the Sword again at full charge.

| Charge source | Charge | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Melee damage | 1% per 10 damage | - |
| Normal block | 5% | Once per 1s |
| Shield block | 5% | Once per 1s (shared with normal blocks) |
| Perfect parry | 10% | - |
| Projectile deflection | 25% | - |
| Chamber | 25% | - |
| Friendly hits | 0% | - |

**Career Button:** Martial Training

Train a 'Melee' unit for 125 Favor, giving them a small but significant permanent buff. Following training paths available:

- Path of the Rain: 25% Physical Ranged Resistance, 10% Movement Speed
- Path of the Hawk: 10% Swing Speed
- Path of Frost: 15% Physical Damage

Uses the Knight of the Old World seal pattern (permanent troop attributes through the career button helper).

## Charge & Techniques

Way of the Sword will be the magic "school" name, four techniques. They are Prayer type (Faith experience, no Winds), with independent cooldowns. Each costs Way of the Sword charge, and can only be used while Way of the Sword is active.

| Technique | Charge | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| Way of the Sword | Requires 100%, refills on entry | - |
| Flight of the Phoenix | 25% | 15s |
| Shadows of Loec | 20% | 20s |
| Path of the Sun | 40% | 30s |
| Path of Falling Water | 50% | 20s |

**Flight of the Phoenix:**

- Penetrating projectile attack like Amber Spear but good, ignoring armor, dealing physical damage.
- 80 damage. Shield and Target Piercing.

**Path of the Sun:**

- Whips allies around you into a frenzy, granting them +20% Physical Damage and +20% Swing Speed. 20s duration.

**Shadows of Loec:**

- Knocks down foes within 5m and gains +75% Movement Speed for 4s, only works on foot.

**Path of Falling Water - GRAND:**

- AOE attack. 60 Physical damage in 4m radius.
