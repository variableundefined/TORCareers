# Council Guard Career

*When the forests of Laurelorn are threatened by Beastmen, Greenskins, or intruding humans alike, the Council Guard march out from Tor Lithanel to war, hewing down their foes with unmatched elven skill and flame-blessed weapons. Only the most trustworthy and skilled of the Toriour are admitted into its ranks, with few exceptions.*

**Premise:** A melee focused career is the biggest missing gap for Eonir at the moment. And melee in Bannerlord is also enjoyable. It also adds their own version compared to say, Glade Lord.

**Fantasy:** Heavy Infantry, non-caster.

- No companion limit boost (It is not a LORD career, unlike Glade Lord), focuses on buffing the Cityborn troop lines,
- with bias toward the left side (Melee) as the right side (Ranged) is already powerful.
- But I do not want the melee buffs to be overwhelming, diverse units is wanted and Eonir can and should field competent infantry and ranged
- Great career ability, spammable and usable in combat.
- **Personal Power Level:** Above KOTOW, below WP of Sigmar / Blood Knight
- **Army Buff Power Level:** Around highest tier KOTOW level (Griffon Sigmarite)
- Elven Fantasy: Standard passive buffs for melee stats, but highest stacking swing speed (2 Nodes) to compensate for slowness of halberd + feels like an elf
- Also must have movement speed. Less than a slayer.
- Synergizes with any melee weapons - as Eonir has some of the best shield enchants. Two-handed and polearms is sorta intended.

**Career Ability:** Judgement of Asuryan

The Asur might have abandoned your kin, but the flame of Asuryan still burns bright in your temple! Ignite your weapon with the flame of Asuryan, gaining +30% 'Fire' damage on your melee strikes and +10% swing speed for 20s. For every level in your highest melee weapon skill, increase its effect by 0.1%. (Ability charges by dealing melee damage.)

**Career Button:** None

**Structure:**

2 - 3 - 2. Standard structure to make a fatter mid game and a good end game rewards. Grey Lord uniquely has 3 - 3 - 1 and I don't think that should be followed.

### Captain (Clan Level 1)

**Toriour:**

- -25% wages for 'Cityborn' troops.
- -30% 'Favor' cost for 'Cityborn' troop upgrades
- +5 'Council Favor' daily, +1 per 50 Charm
- +50% renown from battle
- Judgement of Asuryan charges twice as fast, and starts charged in battle.

**Guardian of Tor lithanel:**

- +20 Personal Hitpoints.
- +10% personal 'Physical' melee damage.
- +10% personal weapon swing speed
- Gain 'Faith' skill per melee kill, scaled by the victim's level.
- Judgement of Asuryan grants 'Cleave'.

### Senator (Clan Level 2)

**Protector of The Silver Tower:**

- +10% 'Ward Save' for all 'Cityborn' troops.
- Wounded Troops heals faster
- +10% swing speed for all 'Cityborn' troops.
- +20 Polearm/Two-Handed skill for all 'Cityborn'
- Judgement of Asuryan also scales with Leadership, and applies to 'Cityborn' within 5m.

**Forge of Rainbow Falls:**

- +20% personal 'Armour Penetration' for melee attacks.
- +10% personal 'Fire' melee damage.
- +25% 'Fire' Resistance for 'Cityborn' troops.
- -25% cost for 'Enchantments'.
- Judgement of Asuryan also scales with Faith, and sets foes you strike burning.

**Guard of the Senate:**

- +35 personal hitpoints
- Hits below 15 damage no longer stagger you.
- +10% personal movement speed
- +1 party move speed on campaign map.
- Judgement of Asuryan also scales with Charm. Kills made during it reduce its cooldown by 2s.

### Queen's Champion (Clan Level 4)

**Temple of Asuryan:**

- +10% 'Fire' damage for all 'Cityborn' troops
- Your 'Cityborn' bonuses also apply to 'Asur' troops
- Kills done by 'Cityborn' troops cause 15% morale damage to enemies.
- +10% 'Physical Damage' for all 'Cityborn' troops
- Judgement of Asuryan doubles in radius, and has a 20% chance on hit to conjure a fire explosion.

**Blades of Elthin Arvan:**

- +15% personal swing speed
- -25% equipment weight
- +15% personal 'Physical' melee damage
- +30% personal 'Fire' and 'Spell' resistance
- Killing a burning enemy in melee inflicts a fire DoT effect on enemies within 3m.

### Hireling Activities

- Practice the same thrust a thousand times (Polearms)
- Run around the army camp in circles (Athletics)
- Meditate to balance your Yenlui (Faith)
- Socialize with the nobles and leaders of the army (Charm)
- Give speeches to encourage the soldiers (Leadership)
