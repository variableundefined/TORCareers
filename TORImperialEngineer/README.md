# Imperial Engineer Career

- **Available For:** Empire
- **Summary:**
  - Powerful and flexible Experimental Munitions for his own guns.
  - Bonuses eventually grow to cover troops
  - Powerful passive and active bonus for Gunpowder troops
  - Improved logistics for Gunpowder troops and Artillery
  - Can turn T3 or above recruits into Iron Company Guards for a reliable source of handgunners.
- Can't learn magic.
- Career Ability can backfire and cause damage.

*Engineers are learned and prestigious men in the Empire whose inventions convenience the lives of many. But more often than not, they turn their attention to ceaseless warfares that plague the Empire, their innovative but unreliable inventions turning the tide of war against humanity's many foes. Though not as profitable or safe as maintaining machines for mines, there is plenty of work, joy and danger for someone who seeks to push the boundary of human sciences.*

*Your aptitude for Engineering meant you advanced rapidly within the Imperial Engineers School, networking with a mixture of alchemists, engineers, and rogue engineers alike. You have decided to take one of your prototypes and test it on the Empire's foe to gain some field experience. Unfortunately, a mix-up meant you went out with someone else's surplus rifle instead.*

**Premise:** A ranged, gunpowder focused career meant to focus on improving your own gunpowder troops. Compared to the Dawi Engineer, its dawi counterpart, the Imperial Engineer will be more destructive, and feels more reckless and "experimental". Both of course focus on units.

**Fantasy:** It's like Waywatcher but Imperial German and with GUNS

- **Skills Synergy:** Engineering and Gunpowder. Both will be essential skills. Melee is de-emphasized or less emphasized.
- **Personal Impact:** I expect personal impact to be pretty decent with Experimental Munition, but you would be expected to have a healer or two on standby because of your reckless nature.
- **Army Buffs / Synergy:** Buffs for Gunpowder troops in general, including Outriders and Foot troops
- **Experimental Munition** will only buffs **non-grenades** line because Grenade is already insanely OP and it is really other munition that help

**Career Ability:** Experimental Munition

Load experimental munitions into your gun. Gain 3 shots, plus 1 additional shot per 50 points in 'Gunpowder'. Choose the ammunition type from the ability wheel. Does not affect grenades. Shots can misfire: Heavy 3%, Scatter 5%, Explosive 8%. (Ability charges by dealing melee or ranged damage.)

Cooldown: 15s. Shots stay loaded until fired or the battle ends. Recharges once the last shot is fired.

Damage to Charge: 800. Up to 120 per hit.

**Ammunition -** Imperial Engineer gains 3 Special Munitions (Current version is reused Sigmar Prayer Icons) that they can switch between. This is loaded by using their CA and they can switch mid load:

- **Heavy Shot:** +100% 'Physical' damage, ignores 50% of armor.
- **Scatter Shot:** Fires 6 musket ball pellets in a spread (5 on repeaters). Each pellet deals 50% of a normal shot's damage (40% on repeaters). Goes to 9 / 7 w/ upgrade. 'Pack It In' increases pellets by 50%. Blunderbusses keep their own scatter.
- **Explosive Shot:** 3m 'Fire' explosion where the shot lands, up to 60% of the shot's damage on single-shot guns, 40% on repeaters (Once per shot). Damage falls off toward the edge, no knockdown. No explosion on blunderbusses.

**Misfires:** Misfire can be one of the following:

- Flash in the Pan (30%) - It does not fire
- Hangfire (30%): Fires 1 second later at where you are aiming. Keeps the loaded ammunition effect.
- Fouled (25%): -60% reload speed for 6s but only happens on the last round. Otherwise it is a Flash in the Pan.
- Burst Barrel (15%): 20 'fire' damage explosion in 2.5m around you. Knocks down everyone caught in it.
- Chain Fire (Replaces Burst Barrel on repeater that has more than 1 round): Burst Barrel Explosion, and fires up to 3 rounds in a wide spread.
- Blunderbusses only roll Fouled or Burst Barrel
- Misfires cancel ammunition effects, except Hangfire
- Whirling Cavalcade of Death increases misfire chance on all ammunition, and misfire severity.
- All misfires are announced in combat log in red. (Misfire - Hangfire)

**Career Button:** Enlist into the Iron Company

Turns a tier 3+ troop into an Iron Company Guard for 5 Prestige and 500 gold. (4 Prestige once discounted with Imperial Logistics). Only in Empire towns. Knights, Bretonnian and non-human troops cannot be converted. Shift-click converts up to 5.

### Apprentice Engineer (Clan Tier 1)

**Imperial Logistics**

- +5 ammunition per ammunition pouch you have equipped.
- -25% wages for 'Ranged' troops, including Outriders.
- -25% 'Prestige' cost for 'Ranged' and 'Engineer' troop upgrades.
- +1 party movement speed on the campaign map.
- Experimental Munition also scales with 'Engineering', and can be used at battle start.

**Customized Weapons**

- +10 personal Hitpoints.
- +10% personal ranged 'Physical' damage.
- +15% personal missile speed.
- Gain 'Engineering' experience for each ranged kill, scaled by the victim's level.
- Experimental Munition gains +2 shots and grants +50% reload speed.

### Associate Engineer (Clan Tier 2)

**Imperial School of Engineers**

- +25% personal 'Fire' resistance.
- +15 personal Hitpoints.
- +6 ammunition per ammunition or buckshot pouch you have equipped, +3 per grenade pouch.
- +25 daily experience for each 'Ranged' and 'Engineer' troop.
- Heavy Shot pierces targets. Gains immunity to knockdown from misfires.

**Imperial Gunnery School**

- +20% party spotting range on the campaign map.
- +10% 'Physical' damage for 'Gunpowder' troops.
- +10% accuracy for 'Gunpowder' troops.
- +6 ammunition per ammunition pouch for 'Gunpowder' troops in your party.
- Scatter Shot fires 9 pellets (7 on repeaters). Each munition shot gives 'Gunpowder' troops in 10m +30% reload for 10s.

**Cannons of the Empire**

- +20% personal reload speed.
- +25% party carrying capacity.
- You may deploy 2 additional Great Cannons without having them in your inventory, +2 artillery limit.
- +10% 'Physical' and 'Fire' resistance for 'Gunpowder' troops.
- Explosive Shot: +1m radius. Doubles 'Gunpowder' and 'Engineering' scaling.

### Master Engineer (Clan Tier 4)

**Leonardo's Legacy**

- +5 Companion limit.
- +20% 'Fire' resistance for you and all troops.
- +25% personal 'Fire' damage.
- Experimental Munition also applies to all Companions wielding a firearm.
- Each munition shot gives 'Gunpowder' troops in 10m +30% reload, +15% damage for 10s.

**Whirling Cavalcade of Death**

- Single-shot guns fire 2 projectiles at 75% damage, excluding blunderbusses.
- +15% 'Physical' damage for 'Gunpowder' troops.
- +10% 'Physical' resistance for 'Gunpowder' troops.
- +10% personal 'Physical' resistance.
- Increases the effectiveness of Experimental Munition. +50% misfire chance, +severity.
  - Heavy Shot = +50% damage.
  - Scatter Shot = +25% damage.
  - Explosive Shot: +25% damage, +25% explosion damage.

### Hireling Activities

- Test-fire prototypes at the firing range (Gunpowder)
- Maintain the army's cannons (Engineering)
- Drill the troops in volley fire (Leadership)
- Patch up your powder burns (Medicine)
- Haggle with the powder merchants (Trade)
